CAT ARCHIVES

Why Cats Love Being Brushed

Bonding through grooming

Cats spend a lot of time grooming themselves, and when you brush them, you're participating in a natural social behavior. Regular brushing strengthens your bond, reduces shedding, and helps prevent hairballs. Most cats come to enjoy the attention and relaxation that comes with a gentle brushing session.

Choosing the right brush

Different brushes work for different coat types. Slicker brushes are great for medium to long-haired cats, while bristle or rubber brushes suit short-haired cats. Start with soft, gentle strokes and always brush in the direction of the fur. Let your cat get used to the brush before longer sessions.

Making brushing a positive experience

Brush your cat when they're relaxed—after a meal or a play session works well. Use treats and praise to create positive associations. Keep sessions short at first and gradually increase as your cat gets comfortable. A calm, patient approach turns brushing into quality time you both look forward to.
